# Code of Conduct

🇫🇷 [Version française](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md)

## Our pledge

In the interest of keeping this project an open and welcoming space, everyone who takes part in it — through an issue, a pull request, a discussion, or any other exchange tied to the repository — pledges to make it a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behaviour

- Show respect for viewpoints and experiences different from your own.
- Accept constructive criticism with an open mind.
- Focus on what best serves the project and its users.
- Be patient — this project is maintained by a single person, in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information (address, email...) without their explicit consent.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to [contact@alithiel31.dev](mailto:contact@alithiel31.dev). All complaints will be reviewed and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The person reporting an incident can count on their report being handled confidentially.

The project maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently suspend any contributor for behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public when someone is representing the project or its community.
